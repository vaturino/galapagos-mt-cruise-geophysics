# Note: permit dredge sites that repeat poor MV1007 dredges (decide before AT53-04)

no glass or no rock. No other permit site is within 2 km of a poor MV1007 dredge.

| **D26** (1.38292 N, 91.18160 W) | 1774 m | MV1007 **D23**, 1946 m | 0.92 km | sediment only, **no rock** (small seamount on lineament 2) | SO158 DR41-A, 17.3 km |

## What the map shows

- **D7:** targets the same summit area where D14 dredged altered basalt with no glass.
  MV1007 D13, dredged from the base of the same seamount about 800 m deeper, did recover
  glass.
- **D25:** sits on the same summit of Big Round Seamount as D22, at almost the same depth
  (1123 vs 1130 m). D22 brought up plenty of rock (150 kg), but all of it altered.
- **D26:** targets the summit of the small seamount, 170 m shallower than D23's flank
  dredge, which recovered only sediment.

## Options for each site (a decision for the cruise planners, not made here)

1. **Keep it as a deliberate re-dredge**, knowing the 2010 result, e.g. if the aim is a
   second attempt on the same edifice.
2. **Move the target on the same edifice**, e.g. to a steeper flank or a fresher-looking
   flow. Check the move stays inside the permit area (PC-103-26, condition 19). New sites
   need a SOLICITUD DE INCLUSION (condition 18).
3. **Swap it for a nearby unsampled target.** `Permit_Sites_Nearest_Previous.csv` and the
   zoom maps (`Dredge_Zoom_A`-`D`) show what is nearby.

Caveats: the 2010 positions are the logged on-bottom and off-bottom points. The actual
dredge path between them is assumed straight. "No glass" is from the sample spreadsheet
and the log description, not a re-examination of the samples.
